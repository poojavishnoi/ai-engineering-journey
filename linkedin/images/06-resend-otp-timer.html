Why can't I tap
"Resend OTP"?
Three reasons.

Resend OTP in 0:29
1 · The first one is on its way
SMS is slow, not lost.
Resend too soon and you get two OTPs. Which one works?
2 · Every SMS costs money
The app pays for each OTP.
A few paise, times millions of logins a day.
3 · Bots press it on purpose
SMS pumping fraud.
Scammers earn a cut of every SMS. The app pays the bill.
The timer is a speed limit.30s → 60s → "Get OTP on call"
